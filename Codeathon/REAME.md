📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product performance. The project demonstrates data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard design to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders monitor key business metrics, identify growth opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profitability trends.
Identify top-performing products and categories.
Understand customer purchasing behavior.
Evaluate business performance across regions.
Track Year-to-Date (YTD) and Year-over-Year (YoY) performance.
Provide actionable insights and business recommendations.

📊 Key Business Insights
Furniture generated the highest sales and profit among all categories.
Total sales exceeded $94M while maintaining a profit margin of approximately 5.5%.
Sales show seasonal peaks during year-end periods.
Growth patterns vary significantly across years, highlighting opportunities for performance stabilization.
A small group of products contributes a significant portion of overall revenue.
💡 Recommendations
Improve profit margins through pricing optimization and cost control.
Prioritize high-performing products and categories.
Leverage seasonal demand through proactive inventory and marketing planning.
Investigate periods of negative YoY growth to identify improvement opportunities.
Focus on customer retention and high-value customer segments.
